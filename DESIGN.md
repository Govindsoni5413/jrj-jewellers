## Brand & Style

This design system expresses timeless Indian artisanal magnificence tailored for high-ticket digital commerce. The aesthetic blends historical royal Indian grandeur with contemporary haute-couture minimalism, delivering an atmospheric, dignified, and trustworthy digital salon experience.

### Brand Personality & Core Virtues
- **Regal Heritage:** Rooted in generations of royal metallurgy, Jadau, Kundan, and bridal craftsmanship without feeling dated.
- **Opulence & Restraint:** Luxuriously rich details balanced by expansive editorial whitespace and airy layouts.
- **Uncompromising Trust:** Clear institutional assurances around purity, certification, and value, seamlessly embedded as prestige hallmarks rather than commercial badges.

### Visual Style
A tailored hybrid of **Heritage Editorial Luxury** and **Warm Tactile Minimalism**:
- Pristine warm ivory and raw silk-inspired cream tones replace stark digital whites.
- Deep regal wine and burgundy establish gravity, drama, and architectural structure.
- Polished metallic gold accents mimic brushed kundan bezels and antique gold foils.
- Framing uses delicate hair-thin outlines, generous tracking, and deliberate vertical pacing to evoke an invitation to a private jewellery viewing.

## Colors

The color palette reflects royal Indian treasuries, heirloom velvet jewel boxes, and radiant golds:

### Color Roles & Distribution
- **Primary (`#4A0E17` / Deep Royal Wine):** Used for commanding interactive focal points, primary action buttons, key brand banners, and prominent header surfaces. Deepened to `#2D080D` for deep footer sections and immersive modal backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` / Antique Lustre Gold):** Used for gold price ribbons, interactive hover highlights, active navigation underlines, rating stars, and hallmark border lines. Tonal variations include `#E5C158` (champagne highlight) and `#C59B27` (deep antique stroke).
- **Tertiary (`#C59B27` / Muted Artisan Gold):** Used strictly for subtle decorative rules, key attribute markers, certification seals, and delicate iconography.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` / Warm Alabaster Ivory):** The predominant background layer across the entire catalog and editorial grid. Supported by `#F5EFEB` for secondary cards, drawer panels, and product detail specs. `#FFFFFF` is reserved strictly for zoomable jewellery showcase plates to maintain true stone color fidelity.
- **Typography & Contrast (`#1A1A1A` / Deep Onyx):** All primary headlines and body copy rely on this deep charcoal rather than pure black to preserve warmth and softness against cream surfaces. Secondary metadata and captions use `#59534E`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates tension between high-fashion editorial craftsmanship and technical e-commerce precision.

### Pairing Strategy
- **Playfair Display (Serif Headings):** High-contrast transitional letterforms evoke bespoke invitation cards and luxury auction catalogues. Used for campaign hero statements, collection titles, price numerals, and section banners.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Microcopy):** Contemporary geometric sans with generous x-height and clear terminals. Delivers effortless legibility for carat specifications, BIS Hallmark identifiers, dimensions, and transactional interfaces.

### Stylistic Directives
- Headline treatments above 24px must maintain a balanced line-height to prevent ascender collisions.
- Subheaders, collection names, and category breadcrumbs frequently use `label-caps-sm` with upper-case transformation and wide tracking (`0.15em`) for an airy, haute-joaillerie feel.
- Pricing utilizes Playfair Display with standard decimal figures, establishing an heirloom prestige feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **Disciplined 12-Column Fluid Architecture** bounded by generous outer gutters that preserve the quiet luxury of white space.

### Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container width of 1440px. Outer margin is 64px (`4rem`), and column gutter is 24px (`1.5rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout. Outer margin reduces to 32px (`2rem`), gutter to 20px (`1.25rem`).
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout. Margin shrinks to 20px (`1.25rem`) with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, allowing product photography to command visual priority.

### Rhythm & Pacing
Vertical spacing between core merchandising modules (e.g., Hero to Featured Curations, Curations to Bridal Gallery) requires substantial breathing space (between 80px and 120px on desktop) to allow each jewellery category to feel like a distinct salon room. Within cards and actionable micro-components, spacing tightens cleanly using the standard 4-point spacing scale.

## Elevation & Depth

To prevent a synthetic digital look, elevation abandons harsh grey drop-shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering**, **Warm Ambient Diffusion**, and **Gold Filigree Tracing**.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Foundation Level (Flat):** Primary canvas surface (`#FAF7F2`) without shadow. All section dividing boundaries use 1px hair-thin borders tinted with antique gold opacity (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`).
2. **Resting Cards (Surface Tier 1):** Solid white or cream surfaces framed with subtle 1px border `rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.08)`. Minimal ambient floor shadow: `0 8px 24px -6px rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.05)`.
3. **Hover & Elevated Cards (Surface Tier 2):** Elevated cards rise smoothly. Shadow increases to a warm, wine-and-amber tinted dispersion: `0 18px 40px -10px rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.12)`. The border transitions to solid `#D4AF37`.
4. **Mega Menus & Modal Salons (Surface Tier 3):** Floated directly over page content with a heavy backdrop filter blur (`12px`) and an ambient glow: `0 24px 64px rgba(45, 8, 13, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft Architectural Shape Language** (`roundedness: 1`).

### Form Principles
- **Crisp Editorial Boundaries:** Standard UI elements (cards, hero banners, text inputs) utilize an intentional 4px border radius (`0.25rem`). This slight softening removes brutalist harshness while retaining the structured, precise cut of architectural jewellery display cases.
- **Pills Reserved for Curated Tags:** Only circular elements, status pills (e.g., "Ready to Ship", "Bestseller"), and floating circular controls (wishlist hearts, carousel chevrons) break this rule, adopting full circular radii to contrast cleanly against structured product frames.
- **Framed Imagery:** Jewellery card media wrappers use precise square (`1:1`) or royal portrait (`4:5`) aspect ratios with crisp 4px corners, evoking custom-mounted picture frames.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Calls to Action
- **Primary Regal Button:** Deep royal wine background (`#4A0E17`), high-contrast ivory text (`#FFFFFF`), `label-caps-sm` typography with uppercase tracking. On hover, background shifts to darker wine (`#2D080D`) with a sharp inner 1px gold border (`#D4AF37`).
- **Secondary Heritage Button:** Transparent background, 1px solid antique gold border (`#D4AF37`), deep wine text (`#4A0E17`). On hover, fills with pale champagne (`#FAF4E6`).
- **Text Link CTA:** Playfair italic or Plus Jakarta Sans bold with an animated antique gold underline spanning 100% width on hover.

### 2. Live Announcement & Gold Rate Ribbon
- Positioned above primary header. Deep wine background (`#2D080D`) with delicate 1px bottom gold stroke.
- Displays live 22K and 24K gold rates per gram, BIS Hallmark guarantee, and global concierge links in clean `label-caps-sm` typography separated by delicate gold diamond glyphed dividers (`✦`).

### 3. Header & Regal Navigation Mega-Menu
- **Navigation Bar:** Warm ivory ground (`#FAF7F2`) with centered royal heritage crest mark, flank-left categories (Bridal, Kundan, Solitaires, Polki, Gold Coins), and flank-right utility icons (Search, Appointment, Wishlist, Bag).
- **Active Navigation Indicator:** Subtle 2px antique gold bar resting directly beneath the category label.
- **Mega-Menu Overlay:** Expansive cream palette pane divided into three thematic columns (Shop by Metal, Shop by Occasion, Curated Collections) with high-fashion campaign photography cards on the right column.

### 4. Jewellery Product Cards
- **Structure:** 4:5 image container with ivory background (`#FFFFFF`), ambient hover zoom (scale: 1.04 over 500ms ease), and quick-view overlay.
- **Metadata Layout:** 
  - Eyebrow: Precious purity label in gold caps (e.g., `18K ROSE GOLD & VVS DIAMONDS`).
  - Title: Playfair Display product name (`headline-sm`).
  - Price: Prominent INR format with transparent breakdown tooltip (Metal weight, making charges, gemstone cost).
- **Wishlist Trigger:** Top-right floating circular icon button with subtle wine-tinted border.

### 5. Trust & Assurance Badges (The Hallmark Row)
- Horizontal 4-up module set against soft ivory silk background (`#F5EFEB`).
- Four pillars: *100% BIS Hallmarked Pure Gold*, *IGI & SGL Certified Diamonds*, *Guaranteed Lifetime Exchange Value*, *Fully Insured Doorstep Delivery*.
- Rendered with fine-line vector icons finished in antique gold foil (`#D4AF37`) accompanied by refined serif micro-headers.

### 6. Form Inputs & Filters
- Understated input boxes with 1px stone border (`#E5DFD7`), transitioning to `#D4AF37` on focus with no heavy outer rings. Labels float with upper-case styling.
- Filter chips for karatage (14K, 18K, 22K) and diamond clarity utilize clean 4px rounded rectangles with wine-tinted checkmarks.

### 7. Opulent Footer
- Deepest wine ground (`#2D080D`) with subtle repeating vintage Mughal jaali geometric pattern at 3% opacity.
- Multi-column architecture featuring bespoke concierge booking, heritage story excerpt, hallmarking education links, and newsletter signup in champagne gold typography.
